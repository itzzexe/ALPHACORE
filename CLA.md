## Why this exists

AlphaCore is published under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE), which means anybody who
runs a modified copy as a network service has to publish their changes. Some
companies cannot accept that, so a
[commercial licence](COMMERCIAL-LICENCE.md) is also offered.

Both of those can only be offered by somebody who holds the rights to *all* of
the code. If a contribution arrived under the AGPL alone, the project could
never include it in a commercially licensed copy — and the alternative, keeping
two versions of the code apart, is how a project quietly stops accepting
contributions.

So the grant below is what makes it possible to accept your change at all.

pull request, for that contribution and every later one you make.
